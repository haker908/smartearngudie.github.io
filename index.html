What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) means improving a website so search engines can understand its pages and people can find useful information through search. SEO cannot guarantee a first-place ranking.

Simple SEO checklist

  • Write a clear, unique title and meta description for every important page.
  • Answer one real reader question thoroughly and accurately.
  • Use a clear page heading and helpful subheadings.
  • Make the site mobile-friendly, fast, secure, and easy to navigate.
  • Link related pages together and use descriptive link text.
  • After publishing, set up Google Search Console and submit a sitemap.

How to begin exploring online work

  1. Pick one skill to practise, such as writing, basic design, video editing, or website building.
  2. Create two or three sample projects that show what you can do.
  3. Use reputable platforms and never pay someone who promises guaranteed work or guaranteed profits.
  4. Keep improving your skill and apply consistently; income is not guaranteed.

On-Page SEO: Improve Each Page

On-page SEO means improving the content and HTML elements on your own pages so visitors and search engines can understand them. Focus on usefulness first; no checklist can guarantee a particular ranking.

On-page SEO checklist

  • Choose one clear topic that answers a real question people search for.
  • Write a unique, descriptive title and a helpful meta description.
  • Use one clear main heading, then organise the article with useful subheadings.
  • Include relevant phrases naturally; do not stuff the page with repeated keywords.
  • Add descriptive alt text to meaningful images and compress large image files.
  • Link to related pages on your website with clear, descriptive link text.
  • Make sure the page is readable on mobile and loads reliably.
  • Write original, accurate content and update it when information changes.

Off-Page SEO: Build Your Website's Reputation

Off-page SEO includes activities outside your website that can help people discover your work and build trust in your site. Relevant editorial links may help search engines discover pages, but paid or manipulative link schemes can put a site at risk.

Ethical off-page SEO checklist

  • Publish useful guides, original examples, checklists, or research worth referencing.
  • Share your articles in relevant communities only when they genuinely answer a question; follow each community's rules.
  • Build relationships with creators and websites in your topic area.
  • Offer guest contributions where they provide real value to that audience.
  • Keep your public author profile and contact information accurate.
  • Avoid buying bulk backlinks, link farms, automated spam comments, and fake reviews.

Remember: off-page SEO takes time. Focus on trust, relevance, and helpful work rather than chasing a large number of links.

Offer Digital Services

Create a portfolio and offer services such as basic website setup, content editing, or SEO audits. Agree on scope and payment terms before starting work.

How to set up payments safely

  1. First qualify for a monetisation programme and create an account using your real details.
  2. Open that service's official Payments section and check which payout methods are available for Pakistan.
  3. Enter your bank details only inside the official, secure account dashboard when requested.
  4. Never publish your bank account number, IBAN, CNIC, password, PIN, or verification codes on this website.

For AdSense, Pakistan's listed payment options include cheque and wire transfer; the exact options shown in your account are the ones to follow. See Google's official payment-method guide ↗
